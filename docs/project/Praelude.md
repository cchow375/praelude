Praelude is a native piano practice and repertoire tracker built with Tauri v2, Rust and React. Christian, an NEC-prep pianist practicing on a Steinway acoustic baby grand, remains its design authority. **The user is the sensor; the app is the memory.** The pianist supplies Clean, Sloppy or Again; the app counts, times, remembers, runs the metronome and organizes scores. It never grades piano audio. Mac is voice-first, with keyboard/mouse too; the separate Assistant remains off and on hold.

The deterministic offline loop is Pieces → local PDF → page-first Score → selected passage → goal/tempo/variants → Start set. Clean streak supports tempo ladders and ordered variants; Total plays records chosen volume without claiming mastery. Custom variants, visibility and named editable routines persist across pieces. Score marks, micro-targets, warmups, calendar, history, notes and exports retain their contracts. A blank installation contains no personal repertoire or teaching library.

**v11.1 practice feedback:** The Rep Counter now builds a luminous core, orbital arcs, a filling ring and progressively richer sparks as the current clean target fills. Sloppy/Again send falling fragments and deepen amber to coral on successive setbacks, capped at three. Set completion gets a larger radial bloom; a fully completed variant chain gets the largest gold finale. Original offline pluck/glass/air sounds rise and gain harmonics with progress, vary their ornaments, descend on setbacks and resolve into richer set/chain chords. Existing practice rules are unchanged: Total plays retains earned volume, and Again preserves variant-stage progress. No added XP penalty or automatic musical judgment.

Rewards follow newly saved attempt IDs. Polling, reopening, undo/redo and history corrections cannot replay them. The first audio-context creation bug is fixed; click/key gestures unlock later spoken rewards. Audio stays optional and cannot block a durable rep write. The effects consume no input, remove themselves and respect reduced motion. Native immediate speech acknowledgement remains separate.

**Studio and the interface:** v11's furnished room, local profile, ten musical ranks × ten divisions, continued Encore progression and 25 earned-coin items remain. Six equipment slots allow one item each. Focus earns 1 XP/600 focused seconds; completed-set session bonuses are cumulative 1/2/4/6 XP at 3/5/7/10 sets. Divisions earn 25 coins; cost is 100 XP in Prelude, +50 per subsequent rank. History corrections reconcile rewards without repeat grants or deleting owned items. These ranks represent commitment, never musical ability. The original CSS/SVG glass interface supports Dark/Light/System, practical keyboard focus, Today launchpad and a cover library with search/sort and reversible Resting. It is a Tauri interface, not SwiftUI Liquid Glass.
