CodaKiller 9 for macOS
========================

What you need
-------------
- An Apple-silicon Mac (M1 or newer)
- macOS 13 or newer
- A PDF of any score you want to practise

Install
-------
1. Drag CodaKiller into the Applications shortcut in this window.
2. In Applications, Control-click CodaKiller and choose Open.
3. If macOS still blocks it, open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
   choose Open Anyway for CodaKiller, then confirm Open.

The extra first-open step is needed because this direct-download build is not
notarized through Apple's paid Developer ID service.

Start practising
----------------
1. Open Pieces.
2. Choose Add Piece.
3. Enter the title and optional composer, choose a PDF, and add it.
4. Open the piece and start a practice set.

A first-ever install for a new Mac user begins blank. Updating or reinstalling
keeps that user's existing library. Your pieces, practice history, recordings,
photos, and settings stay on your own Mac. The app contains no preloaded books,
quotes, scores, practice history, recordings, or photos.

Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions are only needed for hands-free
voice controls. You can use the rest of the app without them.
