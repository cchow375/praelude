Praelude 11 for macOS
====================

What you need
-------------
- An Apple-silicon Mac (M1 or newer)
- macOS 13 or newer
- Your own score PDF

Install
-------
1. Drag Praelude into the Applications shortcut in this window.
2. In Applications, Control-click Praelude and choose Open.
3. If macOS blocks it, use System Settings > Privacy & Security >
   Open Anyway for Praelude, then confirm Open.

This direct-download build is ad-hoc signed, not notarized through Apple's
Developer ID service. An update can renew macOS permissions for files or voice.

Start practising
----------------
1. Open Pieces > Library > Add Piece.
2. Enter a title, choose a local PDF, and add it. Composer/folder are optional.
3. Open the piece in Score and choose a passage in Tricky Sections.
4. Set your goal and tempo, then Start set.
5. Report each attempt with Clean, Sloppy or Again. You are the judge.

The original PDF stays untouched. Use the piece menu to choose custom cover
artwork, organize folders or Put to rest. Resting retains scores and history.

Make a room of your own
----------------------
Studio turns retained practice into a room, musical ranks and earned coins.
Each ten focused minutes earns 1 XP; completed-set session milestones add
bonuses. Rank path explains the exact rules. Furnish shows item prices and
rank requirements; every purchase uses earned coins only. Owned furnishings
and your local display name survive restart. Ranks celebrate commitment,
not musical ability.

Settings > Appearance offers Dark, Light or System. Choose Save to apply.

Your data
---------
A new Mac user starts with a blank library. Updating or reinstalling preserves
that user's existing library and practice history. Scores, covers, recordings,
photos, settings and Studio stay local. No personal scores, practice history,
books, quotes or recordings are preloaded.

No account is required. Email login, cloud sync and friends are not connected;
the Studio display name is local to this Mac.

Microphone and Speech Recognition permission are only needed for hands-free
voice. Keyboard/mouse practice works without them. Praelude never judges piano
audio; optional Listen Back is for your own ears.
